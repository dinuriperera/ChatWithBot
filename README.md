# ChatWithBot
We developed an AI-powered chatbot designed to help users of all age groups find the most suitable laptop based on their individual needs and preferences. The chatbot interacts with users in a simple, user-friendly manner, asking relevant questions to understand their requirements such as preferred usage (gaming, office work, studying), RAM capacity, storage, budget, and more.

The key goal of this system is to assist even those without technical knowledge in selecting the right laptop without the need to visit a physical store. Users can conveniently access the chatbot through our online platform and receive instant, personalized laptop recommendations. This makes the shopping experience smarter, faster, and more accessible to everyone.
